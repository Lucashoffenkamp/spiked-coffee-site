# Featured Roasters Research

## Tala Coffee Roasters — Libertyville, IL
- Small batch specialty roaster, Northern Illinois
- Founded ~2017, started at summer farmers markets
- Locations: Libertyville (834 Liberty Dr), Highwood, Winnetka
- Tagline: "Sweet beautiful coffee"
- Philosophy: Making quality coffee accessible to all
- Brand vibe: Clean, modern, approachable
- Instagram: @talacoffeeroasters

## Chromatic Coffee — San Jose, CA
- Founded 2012 by Hiver van Geenhoven
- Specialty: Micro-lots, novel cultivars, producer direct trade, rare beans
- Location: 460 Lincoln Ave, Suite 10, San Jose (Willow Glen area)
- Won "Best Coffee Roaster" Metro 2025 Best of Silicon Valley
- Featured on Showtime's Billions
- Known for art gallery vibes, innovative sourcing
- Brand vibe: Bold, artistic, colorful, experimental
- Instagram: @chromaticcoffee

## Ruby Coffee Roasters — Nelsonville, WI
- Founded 2013 by Jared and Deanna Linzmeier
- First batches roasted in a garage in rural Portage County
- Nelsonville population: ~154-200 people
- Tagline: "Colorful Coffees"
- Award-winning cafe in Stevens Point
- Philosophy: Seasonal, quality coffees from caring producers
- Known for: Organic offerings, instant creamery line, colorful branding
- Brand vibe: Colorful, rural charm, quality-focused, warm
- Instagram: @rubyroasters
