# Site Review Notes

The site is looking strong. All sections render correctly:
1. Hero - Logo centered, tagline, locations, scroll indicator. The logo image has a slightly visible rectangle edge against the cream background.
2. Story - Asymmetric layout with text left, lifestyle image right, offset cups image. Beautiful.
3. Concept - "One space. Two worlds." with day/night interior panels side by side. Very effective.
4. Dark transition - Gradient from cream to charcoal works well. "All craft. All local." section with coffee bag image.
5. Vision - Three phase cards (Pop-Up, Mobile, Brick & Mortar) with status badges. Truck and storefront images below.
6. Gallery - Masonry grid with truck evening, lifestyle evening, cups, corner store images.
7. Signup - Dalmatian watermark behind "Be part of the beginning." Email form with Join button.
8. Footer - Dark charcoal with logo, nav links, locations, copyright.

- Overall the site is very polished and tells the story effectively
