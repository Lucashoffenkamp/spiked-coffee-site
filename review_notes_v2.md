# Typography Update Review Notes

## Hero Section
- SPIKED COFFEE in pure Cormorant Garamond typography - looks incredible. Wide tracked, light weight, elegant.
- Dalmatian icon mark above the name - subtle and classy
- Thin horizontal rules flanking "CRAFT COFFEE & FINE BEVERAGES" - matches the Spike & Co. inspo perfectly
- Tagline is clean Jost Light, no italic - "Started on the side. Built to last." reads much better
- Location markers are subtle and well-spaced
- No more boxed logo image - pure typography hero is a massive upgrade

## Story Section
- "Rooted in" in Cormorant Garamond light, "connection." in Cormorant italic accent - elegant
- Body text in Jost Light - clean and readable
- "Lucas & Dylan" in Cormorant italic accent - nice sign-off
- Overall the section looks much more refined

## Concept Section
- "One space." in Cormorant Garamond light, "Two worlds." in Cormorant italic accent
- Day/night panels look great with the lighter font weights
- "Craft Coffee" and "Fine Beverages" headings in light weight with tracking

## Overall Assessment
- The typography upgrade is a massive improvement
- Cormorant Garamond gives it that high-end editorial feel matching the Spike & Co. inspo
- Jost Light for body text is clean and modern
- The font-accent class (Cormorant italic) replaces all the cheesy italic spans
- Everything feels cohesive and elevated
